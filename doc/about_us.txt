# **About HikmaProf**

## **AI-Powered Course Creation. Built for Educators.**

HikmaProf is an AI-driven platform that transforms documents, ideas, and research into **complete, classroom-ready courses**—including slides, videos, assessments, and structured learning pathways.

Built under the **HikmaTech Initiative** at **Hamad Bin Khalifa University**, HikmaProf is designed to redefine how education is created and delivered in the AI era.

## **From Content to Complete Courses**

Upload your material—and HikmaProf builds the rest:

* 📊 Professional **presentations (PPTX-ready)**
* 🎥 AI-generated **lecture videos with narration**
* 🧠 Structured **course modules**
* 📝 Auto-generated **assessments**
* 📦 Instant **export (PDF, PPTX, Video)**

Everything aligned, structured, and ready to teach.
